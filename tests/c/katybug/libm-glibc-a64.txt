fused multiply-add sites of exp, log and pow in Debian 12's AArch64 glibc 2.36

Read from libm.so.6 of libc6 2.36-9+deb12u14 (arm64), sha256
3c4cb3be0b974edf05f023f85ab15107fb5afc2687163593d0d4cf8e80c17b39 (the build
libm-sigs.h lists; objdump -d of exp at 0x30840, log at 0x33fc0, pow at
0x34260). Every fmadd, fmsub and fnmsub of the three functions is a line
below (exp 8, log 23, pow 20); there is no fnmadd, fmla or fmls. libm.c writes
each as an fma() call for KB_LIBM_GLIBC_A64 and leaves every other product and
sum unfused, as the code has them. Most of these are outside the
__FP_FAST_FMA branches of the source: gcc contracted them on its own, so
copying the source's explicit fma calls is not enough (musl on AArch64, built
without contraction, has only those).
